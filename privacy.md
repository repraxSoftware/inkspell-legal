# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Inkspell is provided by RepraxSoftware.

Inkspell ("the app", "we") helps you improve your spelling by writing words by hand. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and the choices you have.

## What stays on your device

Inkspell has no accounts and no server of its own. Everything you do in the app is kept only on your device:

- **Your progress.** Which words you have practiced, when they come back, how often you missed them and which letters tripped you up.
- **Your own words** and the sentences you add to them.
- **Your handwriting.** The shapes of the letters you draw are used on your device to read what you wrote. When you tap "That's not what I wrote", the app keeps a few of your letter shapes on your device so it reads your handwriting better. They are never sent anywhere.
- **Your settings,** such as daily limits, word packs, voice and appearance.

The spelling check for words you add uses the dictionary built into your device. Words are read aloud using recordings included in the app, or your device's built-in voices. None of this is sent to us.

All of it is removed when you delete the app.

## What we do not collect

- Your name, email address, phone number or contacts.
- Your location, photos, microphone or camera.
- Your payment details. Purchases are made through Apple, which tells the app only whether you have Inkspell Plus.
- Any analytics about how you use the app.

## Advertising

If you do not have Inkspell Plus, the app shows a small banner ad on some screens. Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC).

- **Ads are not personalized.** The app asks Google only for ads that are not based on your interests or history, and it does not ask for permission to track you across other apps and websites.
- **What Google may receive.** To show an ad, measure it and prevent fraud, Google may receive information from your device, such as its model, operating system, approximate location based on your connection, and whether you viewed or tapped an ad. Google handles this under its own policies: [How Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
- **Ad content.** Ads are limited to content suitable for general and parental-guidance audiences, and we block categories such as gambling, alcohol, sexual content and get-rich-quick schemes.
- **Your choices.** Where the law requires it, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and some US states, the app asks for your consent before showing ads and lets you change your choice later in Settings. Inkspell Plus removes all ads.

## Purchases

Inkspell Plus is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your payment details. The app checks with Apple whether you have an active purchase.

## Children

Inkspell is intended for students and adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, the date at the top will be updated. Significant changes will be noted in the app's update notes.
